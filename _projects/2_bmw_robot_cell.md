> The details of my work at BMW Group are confidential, so only results are shown here.

**Where:** BMW Group, Munich, Germany

**Goal.** Robot systems for production that pick parts from bins on their own, and robots that work safely alongside people.

**What I did.**

- Contributed to a **dual-robot pick-and-place cell**, in which two industrial robots empty bins of parts.
- Worked on a **collaborative robot (cobot)** application in which the robot shares its workspace with people and works alongside them.
- Validated the work in simulation and on real industrial robots, and deployed it with Docker.

**Results.** The dual-robot cell handled **two full bins (~40 left/right parts) autonomously**.
