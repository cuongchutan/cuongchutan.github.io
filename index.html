Open to full-time, long-term positions in robotics software, 3D perception and robot manipulation — available to start as soon as possible.

I am a robotics software engineer with an M.Sc. in Computational Engineering (Elite Program) from FAU Erlangen-Nürnberg. I build perception and manipulation software that runs on real robots. For my master’s thesis at Carl Zeiss, I developed an RGB-only pipeline that reconstructs meshes of unknown, textureless objects without CAD models; on industrial objects it was the most accurate method benchmarked (2.8 mm Chamfer distance) and ran 32% faster than COLMAP. At BMW Group, I built a dual-robot KUKA/FANUC pick-and-place cell with MoveIt2 and behavior trees that handled two full bins (~40 parts) autonomously. Before that, I worked on human motion prediction for safe human-robot interaction at FAU, safety-critical ADAS software on automotive ECUs at Bosch, and LiDAR SLAM and drone design at HUST.
